How you compare

Rough population strength standards by bodyweight — wide individual variance, take as a compass, not a target.

Estimated from your best-ever top set per lift (Epley formula), not a true tested 1RM. Standards assume free-weight barbell lifts — machine or assisted variants (shown under each gauge) usually let you move more load, so treat those readings as optimistic. Standards also don't account for limb length or training age.
